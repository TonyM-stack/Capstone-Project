# Capstone-Project
## 1. Homeopathic Remedies App
### - The Problem
#### There are many websites that sell homeopathic remedies, but there is not one central website where they are all listed with the best remedy for a particular ailment and also rate the efficacy of the remedy along with correct dosage. Also, there needs to be a list of side effects

### - The Solution
#### An app/website that brings all the remedies together in one place and explains the uses and benefits of each remedy as well as efficacy and dosage

It would be necessary to build an API for this

## 2. Trail Condtions App
### - The Problem
####  Mountain bike trails open and close due to weather, maintenance, or other factors.  This app would bring a number of trails and their info to one site

### - The Solution
#### An app that gathers info from various trail managers and gives current riding condtions e.g.(Closed: Too Wet or Open: RideAway).  Also, gives current weather conditions and forecast, open hours, and abiltiy to post photos and a message.

## 3. Tip Calculator App
#### - Create an app that lets users calculate tips automatically. People can scan a receipt, and the tip calculator can take care of the rest.

This is also great for situations when you’re dining with a large group of people, since everyone has their mobile phones with them when they’re out to eat.

The app can calculate what everyone owes based on what they ordered and automatically include the tip amount for each person or group at the table.
